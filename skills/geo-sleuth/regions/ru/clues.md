# Russia clues

Entries use the shared format (`references/clues/README.md`). Only clues whose "Points to" stays inside this country belong here; clues that tell countries apart go into `references/clues/global.md`.

The pack is minimal so far — street-view service preferences are the main content (`regions.py show ru`). Contributions welcome.

## Plates and vehicles

### Russian license plates
- Look for: white plate with black glyphs, one letter + three digits + two letters (or two letters + digits + letters for trailers/taxis), with the region code in the rightmost box (two or three digits, sometimes with a small RUS flag strip); older public-transport plates are yellow with black glyphs
- Points to: Russia (the region code narrows to an oblast/krai/republic — the leading digit gives the federal district)
- Strength: strong (a legible plate with a region code reaches the oblast)
- Counterexamples: similar white/black plates exist in Belarus and Kazakhstan but without the RUS strip and with their own country codes; diplomatic plates are dark blue; transit plates are red-on-white
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Russia

### House number and street plaques on buildings
- Look for: rectangular plaques next to building entrances carrying the street name and house number in Cyrillic (ул., просп., пер., бул. abbreviations), often white on dark blue or white with a colored border; styles are set by the municipality
- Points to: a Russian city; the plaque style plus the street-name abbreviations separate it from most of the rest of the Cyrillic sphere
- Strength: weak (plaque styles overlap with Belarus and parts of Ukraine; use as a down-weight, never to exclude on its own)
- Counterexamples: Soviet-era plaques look the same across the former USSR; modern Moscow plaques differ from the regions
- Sources: https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Адресный_указатель
